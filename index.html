How importing from MyStudio works

In MyStudio: Class schedule → Export → next 14 days, then press Import from MyStudio here and pick that file. Nothing changes straight away: a window lists new Ninjas (with their parent), class changes, and Ninjas who are no longer in the file, and you tick what to apply.

AI marks an Artificial Intelligence class and JR a JR (ScratchJr) class. The JR Ninjas view lists JR Ninjas with notes on what each one is working on. Older spreadsheet files (Name / Day / Time Slot, or the weekly Session Schedule) also work.
